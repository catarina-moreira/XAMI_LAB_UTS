Explainability is ultimately an interaction problem: the best model insight is worthless
if it cannot be communicated to, explored by, and trusted by a human. This theme designs
the interfaces and immersive environments where human-machine collaboration actually
happens.

![Persuasive, human-centric AI](/assets/project/Persuasive.png)

## Immersive analytics and XR for health

We build virtual and augmented reality systems for clinical work: immersive virtual
colonography for colon growth diagnosis (with liquid-error-aware segmentation), augmented
reality adaptations for collaborative laparoscopy, and immersive analytics for oncology
patient cohorts. Alongside these systems we study the ergonomics of medical XR — how
eye tracking and XR can improve X-ray diagnostics and reading-room conditions.

## Interaction techniques and collaboration in XR

Fundamental interaction research underpins these applications: hands-free navigation
using head motion, gaze-hand steering for travel and multitasking in virtual
environments, shared-perspective avatar techniques for remote collaboration, and studies
of how avatar size, proximity and visual detail shape user experience and trust —
including engagement and social interaction in fully virtual conferences.

## Adaptive and explainable interfaces

Intelligent interfaces should adapt to the human, not the other way around. We design
emotionally adaptive virtual health assistants driven by cognitive and emotional state
predictions, survey and design explainable user interfaces for intelligent healthcare
systems, and develop human-machine teaming approaches for professional training.
