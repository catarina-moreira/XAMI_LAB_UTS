XAMI stands for supporting human-machine collaboration and co-evolution. This theme is
where that collaboration is designed: the interfaces, immersive environments and virtual
agents through which people explore, question and trust intelligent systems. The best
model insight is worthless if it cannot be communicated to the human who needs it.

## Immersive and extended reality for health

We build virtual and augmented reality systems for clinical work: immersive virtual
colonography for diagnosing colon growths, augmented reality adaptations for
collaborative laparoscopy, and immersive analytics for oncology patient cohorts. We also
study how eye tracking and XR can improve X-ray diagnostics and the ergonomics of
radiology reading rooms.

## Interaction and collaboration in virtual environments

Fundamental interaction research underpins these systems: hands-free navigation using
head motion, gaze-hand steering for travel and multitasking in virtual environments, and
shared-perspective avatar techniques for remote collaboration. We study how avatar size,
proximity and visual detail shape user experience and trust, including engagement and
social interaction in fully virtual conferences.

## Adaptive and explainable interfaces

Intelligent interfaces should adapt to the human, not the other way around. We design
emotionally adaptive virtual health assistants driven by cognitive and emotional state
predictions, explainable user interfaces for intelligent healthcare systems, and
human-machine teaming approaches for professional training, connecting this theme
directly to the lab's explainability mission.
