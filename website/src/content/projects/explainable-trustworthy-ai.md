This is the foundational theme of the XAMI Lab and the core of our mission: making
machine intelligence explainable, transparent, fair and understandable to the humans who
must act on its predictions. Modern predictive models are deployed as black boxes whose
internal representations no person can inspect. We build the algorithms, the causal
theory and the evaluation science needed to open those boxes responsibly.

## Counterfactual and causal explanations

People understand decisions through "what if" questions: what would I have to change for
my loan application to be accepted? Our research builds counterfactual explanation
algorithms and grounds them in causality, because correlation-based explainers are
sensitive to sampling bias and can be more misleading than the black box itself. This
line includes interpretable probabilistic models such as LINDA-BN, causal inference from
observational data, and Bayesian causal discovery to support real decision making.

## Evaluating explanations

An explanation is only worth trusting if it is faithful to the model, stable across
similar cases, and actionable for the person receiving it. We are among the leading
groups in the evaluation of XAI: fidelity and stability metrics for post-hoc explainers,
functionally-grounded evaluation guidelines, and large-scale benchmarking of
counterfactual algorithms from white boxes to black boxes.

## Contestable, fair and robust AI

Trustworthy AI must allow the people it affects to question it, and must withstand
manipulation. We study how AI systems can be made contestable by design, how to detect
and mitigate bias for fair machine-learned decisions, and how models behave under
adversarial attack, including imperceptible attacks on tabular data and benchmarks for
adversarial robustness.

## Explainable predictive process analytics

A flagship application of this theme: predicting future states of business processes
from event logs, with explanations. We pioneered interpretability in this space, from
interpretable process predictive models and XAI-enabled model inspection to
milestone-aware counterfactuals and multi-level explanations for process outcomes,
applied in healthcare, government, finance and industry.
